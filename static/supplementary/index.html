Hand Motion Explorer

Searchable, filterable card-grid viewer over a 20-clip subset of RoMo Hands. Each card opens a full MANO viewer with timeline scrubber, five-level captions, taxonomy labels and per-clip quality scores.

Synchronized Motion Grid

Single-scene 3D viewer rendering all 20 representative bimanual MANO sequences in a synchronized grid. Click a tile to focus, scrub and inspect one motion at a time.

Generative Method Comparisons

Side-by-side MANO viewers comparing ground-truth motion against generative baselines (MDM, MoMask, MotionMillion, Kimodo) across five caption levels.

Generative Method Benchmark

Quantitative evaluation of generative baselines: taxonomy-aware semantic retrieval (R@K, MM-Dist, CLIP), distributional metrics (FID, diversity), and physical plausibility.
